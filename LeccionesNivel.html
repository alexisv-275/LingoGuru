Verb To Be

The verb "to be" is one of the most important verbs in English. You'll use it all the time to describe states, identities, and conditions.

  • check_circle Use of the verb "to be" in the present tense
  • check_circle Affirmative, negative, and interrogative sentences
  • check_circle Contractions and colloquial forms

MAIN RULE

am → I · is → he, she, it · are → you, we, they
Subject + am/is/are + complement
